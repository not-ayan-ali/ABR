ABR Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026
Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Who we are

ABR ("we", "us") is a mobile app for reading serialized Urdu novels, published by Ahsan bin Ramzan. You can contact us at ahsanbinramzan.pk@gmail.com.

2. Summary

ABR does not require an account, email address or password. We do not show ads and we do not sell your data. We ask only for a display name and keep only the information needed to make the app work.

3. Information we collect

  • Display name. The name you type the first time you open the app. You can change it later in Profile.
  • Device identifier. A unique ID created for your device or app installation. It lets us keep your favorites, reading progress and ratings linked to your device without a login.
  • Reading activity. The novels you add to favorites, the episodes you read, how far you read (percentage and position), and when you last read.
  • Ratings. The 1 to 5 rating you give a novel, and when you gave it.
  • Settings stored only on your device. Your theme, reading text size and your name.
  • Technical information. When the app connects to our servers, our hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address in standard server logs.

We do not collect your email address, phone number, address, location, contacts, photos or files, camera or microphone data, payment information, or advertising ID.

4. How we use your information

  • To provide the features of the app: continue reading, favorites and ratings.
  • To show the average rating of each novel.
  • To give the publisher combined statistics, such as the number of reads, popular categories and average ratings.
  • To keep the app secure and fix problems.

We do not use your information for advertising. The app does not show your name to other readers.

5. Who we share information with

  • Service providers. We use Supabase for database and file storage hosting. Supabase stores data on our behalf.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law or a valid legal process.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

6. Storage and security

Your data is stored on Supabase servers in South East Asia (Singapore), which may be outside your country. Data is encrypted while it travels between the app and our servers (HTTPS). Because the app has no login, your activity is linked to your device identifier. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while the app is offered or until you delete it. You can delete all data linked to your device in the app under Settings > "Delete my data", or by emailing ahsanbinramzan.pk@gmail.com. Uninstalling the app removes the data on your device but does not automatically remove the data on our servers, so please use "Delete my data" first.

8. Your choices and rights

You can change your name in Profile, delete your data, and ask us for access to or correction of your data by emailing ahsanbinramzan.pk@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.

9. Children

ABR is not directed at children under 13 (or a higher age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it. Novels may show an age rating.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the "Last updated" date. If you keep using the app after a change, the new policy applies.

11. Contact

Ahsan bin Ramzan — ahsanbinramzan.pk@gmail.com
